# Research background, not operating instructions

- NoVa-PVC: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40392172/
- TREAT-PVC: https://www.jacc.org/doi/full/10.1016/j.jacep.2026.01.011
- Median nerve research: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32502412/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37133932/
- Clinical scoping review: https://doi.org/10.1080/09638288.2024.2313123

The first two studies investigated 20 Hz; the Tourette research used 10–12 Hz.
74% of studies reporting frequency in the scoping review used 20 or 25 Hz.
Frequency alone does not imply equivalent biological or electrical effects.
This instrument is strictly for logic measurement and onboard speaker audio tests.
